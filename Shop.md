# 🛒 Shop

Servers sell custom roles and access to private channels. Prices are
set by admins.

---

## Browse `/shop`
You'll see two sections:
- **🎭 Roles** — cosmetic roles you buy once
- **📺 Channels** — access to private channels

---

## Buying
For a **role**: `/buy-item type:roles slot:1`

For a **channel**: `/buy-item type:channels slot:1`


Match the slot number from `/shop`.

---

## What Happens to the Money?

Your payment goes into the **server's treasury**.

That money funds things like:
- `/work` payouts for everyone
- Server events
- Grants from the government

So buying from the shop isn't just for you — it keeps the economy moving.

---

## No Refunds

Once bought, it's yours — but the money stays in the treasury. Admins
can't undo a purchase. Choose carefully.
