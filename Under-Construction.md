# 🚧 Under Construction

Sometimes systems go down for updates. When that happens, you'll see:

> 🚧 The [system] is under construction. Check back soon.

This means the bot's developer is working on that part. **You can't use
its commands** until they're done.

The affected systems will be back online shortly. There's nothing you
need to do.

**Tip:** Try a different command in the meantime. The rest of the bot
usually keeps working.
