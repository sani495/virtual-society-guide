## 🧪 Alpha & Beta Testers

Virtual Society is being built in public. Our alpha and beta testers
get early access to new features and help shape the bot.

**Want to help test?** Join [TwiceDice Support](https://discord.gg/Fwavq3GNxs) and ask.

**Alpha testers** get:
- Early access to new systems
- A badge role
- Direct line to the developer

**Beta testers** get:
- Access to previews before public release
- A badge role

Neither role gives any bot advantages — they're recognition only.
