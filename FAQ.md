# ❓ FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

---

**Why can't I work?**

Three possibilities:
- You don't have a job → `/apply slot:1`
- You're on cooldown → wait
- Your server's treasury is empty → tell an admin. They can run `/fund`
  to refill it.

---

**How do I get more money fast?**

- `/work` on cooldown
- Buy from the shop (helps the whole server)
- Watch for promo codes — ask your server admin
- Get promoted (higher levels pay more)

---

**Can I rob someone?**

Not yet. The crime system is planned.

---

**How do I get a custom role?**

Your server's shop sells them. Check `/shop`.

---

**How do I get my server partnered?**

Contact the government staff in [TwiceDice Support](https://discord.gg/Fwavq3GNxs)

---

**Why is my paycheck smaller today?**

Your level might have changed, or your job slot was edited by an admin.
Check `/job-info`.

---

**Can I have more than one job?**

No. One at a time. `/quit-job` first if you want to switch.

---

**What happens if I leave a server?**

Your wallet stays with you. Your job is gone. Reapply when you join a
new server.

---

**Is any of my data public?**

Your `/balance`, `/profile`, and `/job-info` are visible to anyone with
those commands. Your bank data is read-only for others.

---

**Did I lose money?**

Probably not. Money isn't stored per-server — it's attached to your
Discord account. If something looks wrong, report it in the support
server.
