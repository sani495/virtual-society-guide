# 💼 Jobs

## Getting a Job

Run `/job-list` to see what's available. Each server sets up to 5 job slots.

To take one:
/apply slot:1

Replace `1` with whichever slot you want.

---

## Working

Once you have a job:

/work
You earn money every shift. There's a cooldown (usually an hour) — you
can't spam it.

**Your paycheck comes from the server's treasury.** If the treasury is
empty, work fails. Tell an admin.

---

## Leveling Up

Every `/work` gives 1 XP. Every X XP, you level up. Higher levels earn
more per shift.

Your level is automatic. Check it with:

/job-info

---

## Quitting

Want out?

/quit-job

You can apply for a new job immediately after.

---

## Can't Work?

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| "You don't have a job" | `/apply slot:N` |
| "You're tired" | Wait for the cooldown |
| "Server treasury is too low" | Ping an admin |
| "Your job was removed" | Server deleted your slot — reapply |
