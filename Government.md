# 🏛️ The Government

The government is a specific Discord server that runs the **global
economy**. It's connected to [TwiceDice Support](https://discord.gg/pzSdaFzR83).

---

## What the Government Does

- Holds the **global bank** (`/global-bank`) — money every server shares
- Issues **loans** to servers
- Creates **promo codes** for servers to redeem
- Partners with servers in the network
- Sets global rules during events (coming soon)

---

## See It 
/government

Shows:
- Government server name
- Global bank balance
- Number of governors
- List of partnered servers

---

## Who Runs It

**Governors** — trusted staff appointed by the bot owner. They can:
- Grant money to servers
- Create and delete promo codes
- Partner new servers

The bot owner always has governor powers, even if they're not listed.

---

## Partnered Servers

Servers can be **partnered** with the government. Partners get:
- Priority loans
- First access to promo codes
- A spot on the government's partner list

Want your server partnered? Contact the government staff.

---

## Can I Join the Government?

Governors are hand-picked. If you're active, helpful, and trusted, you
might get invited.

There's no application. There's no queue. Be the kind of person we'd
want running a bank.
