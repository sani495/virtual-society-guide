# 🚀 Getting Started

Your first 5 minutes in Virtual Society.

---

## 1. Check your wallet

/balance - You start with **🪙 500 cash**. That's your seed money.

You'll see two numbers:
- **Cash** — spendable right now
- **Bank** — safer, but takes a step to access

---

## 2. Get a job
/job-list - This lists every job on this server. Pick one you like.

/apply slot:1 - Done. You're employed.

---

## 3. Work
**Your paycheck comes from the server's treasury.** If the treasury is
empty, you can't get paid. Not your fault. Tell an admin — they can use
`/fund` to refill it.

---

## 4. Buy stuff
/shop - Servers sell custom roles and access to private channels. Prices vary.

/buyitem type:roles slot:1 - 
If you have enough cash, you own it.

---
