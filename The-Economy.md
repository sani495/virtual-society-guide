# 📈 The Economy

Virtual Society isn't just a pile of individual wallets. It's a
connected system. Here's how it actually works.

---

## Money Has a Source

Most economy bots print money out of nowhere. This one doesn't.

**Every dollar comes from somewhere and goes somewhere.**

- `/work` takes money from the **server's treasury** and gives it to you
- `/buy-item` takes your cash and puts it in the **server's treasury**
- The government can **print money** into the system via grants and promos

If the treasury runs dry, work stops. That's intended. Servers have to
earn to pay.

---

## Three Layers of Money

1. **You** — personal Cash and Bank
2. **The Server** — a shared treasury (`/server-bank`)
3. **The World** — one global bank across all servers (`/global-bank`)

Money flows between all three.

---

## Why Work Fails

If your server's treasury runs low, `/work` stops paying. This isn't a
bug — it's the economy.

To refill a treasury:
- Players buy shop items
- Admins run `/fund` (this prints new money)
- Governors issue grants
- The government sends promos

Tell an admin if your server is dry.

---

## Loans (Server Level)

Servers can borrow from the global bank. Three loans max. Server admins
handle this — players don't take loans personally.

Money borrowed goes into the server treasury to fund work and events.
Repay it with `/loan-pay`.

---

## Promo Codes

Governors create promo codes. Server admins redeem them. The money
prints into the server's treasury.

You don't run `/redeem` yourself — an admin does. But when they do, your
server benefits.

---

## 📈 Server Levels & Credits

Servers are not just static places — they level up and earn rewards.

### How Servers Level Up
Every time a member successfully uses `/work`, the server earns **1 Server XP**. 
Once the server reaches 1,000 XP, it levels up, the XP resets to 0, and the treasury deposit limit increases.

*   **Level 1:** Max treasury deposit is 🪙 100,000
*   **Level 2:** Max treasury deposit is 🪙 200,000
*   **Level 3:** Max treasury deposit is 🪙 300,000
*(And so on...)*

### What are Credits?
Every time a server levels up, it earns **1 Credit**. Credits are a special government currency that server admins can spend using `/credits`.

**How to spend Credits:**
*   **Forgive Debt (1 Credit):** Wipes up to 🪙 300,000 off your server's loan debt.
*   **Buy Loan Slot (2 Credits):** Removes 1 from your `Loans Taken` count, allowing you to borrow again even if you hit the 3-loan limit.

---

## TL;DR

Money is finite. It moves. It doesn't appear from nowhere. Servers that
earn, survive.
