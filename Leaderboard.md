# 🏆 Leaderboards

See who's on top of the network.

## `/leaderboard`

Shows three things:

- **Top 3 servers** by treasury balance
- **Top 3 users** by net worth
- **Your own rank** and net worth

Two buttons let you flip between the **Servers** page and the **Users** page.

## How Ranking Works

- **Servers** are ranked by their treasury balance (`/banks`)
- **Users** are ranked by net worth (wallet + bank)

Both lists update every few minutes, so your rank may lag behind your latest transaction.

## Tips

- Build up your bank balance to climb the user leaderboard
- Server admins can grow the server's rank by funding the treasury with `/fund`
