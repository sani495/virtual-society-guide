# 💰 Money & Banking

## Your Two Wallets

You have **Cash** and **Bank**.

- **Cash** — on you. Used for shop purchases and transfers.
- **Bank** — safer. Takes a command to move money in or out.

There's no interest yet. Both are just storage right now.

---

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/balance` | See cash, bank, and total |
| `/balance user:@someone` | Peek at someone else's wallet |
| `/deposit amount:500` | Move cash → bank |
| `/withdraw amount:500` | Move bank → cash |
| `/pay user:@someone amount:500` | Send cash to another player |

You can type `all` in place of a number:

/deposit amount:all

/withdraw amount:all


---

## Tips

- **Deposit before buying big things** — keeps your cash from tempting robbers (crime system coming soon)
- **Pay is instant** — no fees, no tax between players
- **Transfers go both ways** — no minimum
